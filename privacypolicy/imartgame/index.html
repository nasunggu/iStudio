Privacy Policy

iStudio ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our mobile game Little Shopper Supermarket (formerly "Mart Game", the "App"). By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy is intended to reflect the technologies currently integrated into the App, including Google AdMob and Unity Ads (served through Google AdMob mediation). The App is designed for children and families. Ads are configured for child-directed / non-personalized treatment. We do not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and do not use Google UMP consent forms for personalized advertising or tracking. This document is provided for general informational purposes and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional for compliance in the jurisdictions where your App is distributed.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account using your real name, email address, or phone number to play the App. We do not use Firebase or other cloud account systems in this App, and we do not operate our own servers that receive gameplay data.

1.1 Information stored locally on your device

The App stores some information directly on your device in order to provide gameplay features and save your progress. This information is not sent to us. It may include:

  • App settings, such as sound effects (SFX) and background music (BGM) on/off preferences
  • Game progress, such as earned in-game coins, player level, and which foods or items have been unlocked
  • Gameplay-related flags, such as which items were unlocked by watching rewarded ads and whether an unlock animation has already been shown
  • Advertising-related local values, such as the last time an interstitial advertisement was shown (used for cooldown timing)

Deleting the App or clearing its local storage usually removes this locally stored information, subject to your device and operating system behavior.

1.2 Advertising (Google AdMob and Unity Ads) — child-directed and non-personalized

The App may display advertisements (banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads) using Google AdMob. Through AdMob mediation, some ads may be served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Ad requests are configured as follows:

  • Tag for Child-Directed Treatment (TFCD): enabled, and passed to mediation partners such as Unity Ads
  • Max Ad Content Rating: G
  • Publisher Privacy Personalization: Disabled (non-personalized ads)

Accordingly:

  • We do not use interest-based / personalized advertising or behavioral advertising.
  • We do not request ATT (IDFA / tracking permission) on iOS.
  • We do not present Google UMP forms for personalized-ad consent.

When ads are requested or shown, Google, Unity, and their advertising partners may process technical information related to the device and network (for example IP address, device type and model, operating system, coarse location-level signals derived from IP address, device identifiers where available and permitted, and ad interaction information such as impressions and taps) for the purposes of ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement, under their own policies and child-directed treatment. Relevant resources include:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies
  • AdMob & child-directed treatment
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

1.3 Information we do not intentionally collect directly

We do not ask you to enter your real name, postal address, phone number, email address, or payment card details in order to play the App. The App does not offer in-app purchases, chat, social features, or user-generated content. We do not use the App to collect your precise GPS location, and the App does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or photos. The App does not currently include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or social login.
