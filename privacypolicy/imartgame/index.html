1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account using your real name, email address, or phone number to play the App. We do not use Firebase or other cloud account systems in this App.

1.1 Information stored locally on your device

The App stores some information directly on your device in order to provide gameplay features and save your progress. This may include:

  • App settings, such as SFX / BGM on/off preferences
  • Gameplay-related flags, such as which in-game shop items were unlocked via rewarded ads (stored per item button)
  • Advertising-related local values, such as the last time an interstitial advertisement was shown (used for cooldown timing)
  • Other in-game progress or state that the App saves locally as features are expanded

Deleting the App or clearing its local storage usually removes this locally stored information, subject to your device and operating system behavior.

1.2 Advertising (Google AdMob) — child-directed and non-personalized

The App may display advertisements using Google AdMob (for example banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads). Ad requests are configured as follows:

  • Tag for Child-Directed Treatment (TFCD): enabled
  • Max Ad Content Rating: G
  • Publisher Privacy Personalization: Disabled (non-personalized ads)

Accordingly:

  • We do not use interest-based / personalized advertising.
  • We do not request ATT (IDFA / tracking permission) on iOS.
  • We do not present Google UMP forms for personalized-ad consent.

When ads are requested or shown, Google and its advertising partners may process technical information related to the device and network (for example IP address, device type, coarse location-level signals, and other technical signals needed for ad serving), under Google’s policies and child-directed treatment. Relevant resources include:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies
  • AdMob & child-directed treatment

1.3 Information we do not intentionally collect directly

We do not ask you to enter your real name, postal address, phone number, or payment card details in order to play the App. We do not use the App as a dedicated feature to collect your precise GPS location. The App does not currently include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or social login.
